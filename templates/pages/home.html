Exhibitions every season.

A new edition every few months. Not a permanent museum — a gathering that arrives, works, and is documented.

An open call.

Send a painting or a work. Sun Boats reviews every submission and invites selected pieces into the edition.

A week of Egypt.

The same week holds the hang and the country: pyramids, Nile, Minya, Cairo halls. They belong to the exhibition.
